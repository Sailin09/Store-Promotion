Store Promotion

Organic content for independent Etsy shops.

Store Promotion is a private promotion management system for approximately 30 independent Etsy shops. It organizes product information and prepares Pinterest content while keeping each shop's data separated.

Product informationManage product facts, images, categories and shop relationships.
Content generationCreate platform-specific Pin copy and content variants from approved product information.
Queue and schedulingReview, queue and schedule eligible content for organic promotion.
Official API publishingPublish through Pinterest's official API after the account owner completes OAuth authorization.

Privacy and authorization

Store Promotion does not ask for a Pinterest password. Pinterest access is granted through OAuth and is limited to the permissions needed by the enabled features. The system only uses data that the account owner authorizes.

Read the Privacy Policy for details about data, storage, authorization revocation and deletion requests.

Developer

Store Promotion is maintained by Sailin09 for personal use with independently operated Etsy shops. For questions or privacy requests, contact the developer through the project repository.
