Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Store Promotion uses information while it manages organic Pinterest content for independently operated Etsy shops. Store Promotion is maintained by Sailin09 for personal use.

Information we use

  • Pinterest account data: OAuth account identifiers, account and board information, and publishing results that Pinterest makes available under the permissions approved by the account owner.
  • Etsy product information: publicly available listing information such as titles, descriptions, categories, prices, listing URLs and image URLs, plus shop and product relationships needed to route content correctly.
  • Promotion data: generated content variants, queue and schedule details, publication identifiers, timestamps and operational status.
  • Technical data: limited logs needed to operate scheduled jobs, diagnose failures and protect the service. Secrets and access tokens are not written to the GitHub repository.

How information is used

We use this information to organize products, generate and review Pin content, route content by product category, queue and schedule publication, publish through Pinterest's official API, and record publication status. We do not use Pinterest credentials to log in, and we do not ask for or store Pinterest passwords.

OAuth and permissions

Pinterest access is granted through Pinterest OAuth. Access is limited to the permissions required for the enabled Store Promotion features. You can revoke access at any time from your Pinterest account settings or through the connected-app controls provided by Pinterest. Revoking access stops future API calls, although operational records already created may remain until deleted under this policy.

Storage and protection

Operational data is stored in Supabase/PostgreSQL. GitHub stores source code, documentation and workflows. GitHub Actions runs approved deployment and verification jobs. OAuth tokens and other secrets are kept in protected secret storage or service configuration and are not committed to GitHub. No system can guarantee absolute security, but access is limited to the project owner and the services required to operate the system.

Service providers

  • Pinterest: provides OAuth and the official API used for authorized Pin publishing.
  • Supabase: provides the database and related backend services.
  • GitHub and GitHub Actions: provide source control, deployment and verification workflows.
  • Etsy: is the source of publicly available product information used by the system. Store Promotion does not request an Etsy customer password through this site.

These services may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Store Promotion does not sell personal information or sell or share data with unrelated third parties.

Data retention and deletion

Information is retained only while it is needed to operate the promotion system, maintain publication history, or meet security and audit needs. The developer may delete connected account data, tokens and operational records when they are no longer needed. To request deletion of information associated with a Pinterest connection or product record, contact the developer through the project repository and include enough detail to identify the request. We will verify the request and remove the applicable data where reasonably possible, subject to records that must be retained for security or legal reasons.

Children

Store Promotion is a business tool and is not directed to children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the system or its integrations change. The current version will remain available at this URL. For privacy questions, authorization revocation assistance or deletion requests, contact the developer through the Store Promotion GitHub repository.
